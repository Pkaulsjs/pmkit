Paper-Trading Prediction Markets

Before any strategy touches capital, it should survive simulation. Paper trading against live order books (not historical candles) is the closest thing to real execution without funds.

The minimal loop

from pmkit.paper import PaperLedger
from pmkit.clob import best_ask

ledger = PaperLedger(starting_cash=1000.0)
token = "your_token_id"

ba, size = best_ask(token)
if ledger.buy(token, shares=10, price=ba):
    print("filled", ledger.equity())

What simulation cannot show you

  • Your own market impact - simulated fills assume the book absorbs you
  • Latency - by the time your order lands, the price moved
  • Resolution disputes - UMA outcomes can surprise

Discount accordingly: if a strategy barely profits on paper, it loses live.

A practical rule

Run 100+ simulated fills across different market conditions before considering real stake. pmkit includes a tested ledger engine for exactly this.
