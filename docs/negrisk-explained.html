Polymarket NegRisk, Explained

Some Polymarket events have N mutually exclusive outcomes (elections, brackets, score lines). Each sub-market trades YES/NO independently, but the group shares one constraint: exactly one outcome resolves YES.

The constraint

If exactly one of N outcomes resolves YES, rational pricing means the YES prices sum to 1.00. The negRisk flag on Gamma markets marks these groups.

The arbitrage math

Let S = sum of YES prices across the group.

  • S > 1: buy NO on every sub-market. Cost = N - S. Exactly one NO fails, the rest pay (N-1). Profit = S - 1.
  • S < 1: buy YES on every sub-market for S total; one pays 1. Profit = 1 - S.

Why you will rarely capture it

Live data from August 2026: a full scan found 61 groups deviating over 2 percent - and after order-book depth checks, essentially zero were executable at meaningful size. Resting orders on resolved events, 3-share bids, stale midpoints. The bots get there first; the books that look wrong are usually just dead.
