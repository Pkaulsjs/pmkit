Scanning Polymarket for Arbitrage: What Actually Works

Field data: August 2026, 2,100+ binary markets and 3,000+ events scanned.

The two scans

Binary: flag markets where YES + NO is below 1 minus fees. Buy both sides, collect $1 at resolution.

Negative risk: in a mutually-exclusive event with N sub-markets, if the sum of YES prices exceeds 1, selling NO across all sub-markets nets sum minus 1.

Results from a live scan

  • Binary: 0 spreads of 0.5 percent or more in 2,100 markets
  • NegRisk: 61 midpoint deviations over 2 percent - and after order-book validation, effectively 0 executable

Why they were phantom

  • Resolved events with never-cancelled resting orders (zombie books)
  • Best bids 3 shares deep - one market order eats it
  • Stale midpoints on markets with no recent trades

The takeaway

Polymarket long tail is bot-saturated. Scanners are still useful - as market-quality maps, not money printers. Full working scanners: pmkit.
